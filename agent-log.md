## 2026-08-24 — Season refunds get a WHO and a WHEN axis, and the terms finally appear where the money is taken
- **Situation:** Sam set the policy "no refund policy for groups and season." The code said otherwise: `fallRefundPolicyFor()` returned `"full"` for any cancellation before the first Sunday. Worse, exploring the point of sale turned up that **no refund terms were stated anywhere a buyer could see them** — not on `/fall`, not in `FallRegistrationForm`, not in `fall-season-confirmation.ts` — while `fall-cancellation.ts` told parents "that's the policy you agreed to at registration." Seven families had already paid $225 each under terms that existed only in a lib file. Sam also cancelled Christina Doukeris's Wednesday group (venue too far from Potomac), set Kennedy Regner's trial as a prorated payment credited toward the season, reported all drop-in sessions deleted, and asked for camp off `/schedule`.
- **Decision:** Two axes, because one boolean could not carry the policy. **WHO** (`reason`): a parent withdrawing gets nothing back; **NGA cancelling gets the undelivered sessions prorated**, whatever terms they bought under — Sam chose this over a blanket no-refund, and it is the defensible half. **WHEN** (`registeredOnIso` vs a new `FALL_NO_REFUND_EFFECTIVE_FROM`): forward-only, so the seven already-paid families keep the handling they bought under and nobody's terms change after the money moved. An **undated row falls back to the older, more generous rule on purpose** — never apply stricter terms to a row we cannot date. `FALL_NO_REFUND_EFFECTIVE_FROM` is asserted to be on/after the day the on-page terms ship, so the constant cannot silently predate the disclosure it depends on. Proration rounds UP, in the parent's favour, and is capped at what they paid. The terms now render on `/fall`, next to the submit button, and in the confirmation email. The cancellation email's "policy you agreed to" claim was **removed rather than kept** — it was not true for the legacy cohort and asserting agreement nobody gave is the kind of thing this repo's ethics rule exists to stop.
- **Risk:** This is the payments surface, so IPAV was followed strictly: the spec was written first, **7 of 12 cases failed before the implementation**, and the cutoff comparison was **mutation-verified** (inverting `>=` to `<` turned 3 cases red; restored). The route was the subtler bug — it computed its refund decision BEFORE looking up the row and then passed that as an explicit `refund:` override, which would have (a) previewed the wrong answer for exactly the families whose terms differ and (b) suppressed the engine's own row-aware policy on every live call; it now fetches first and forwards only a genuine operator override. `issueRefund` gained an optional amount for the prorated path; omitted, it still refunds the whole charge, so the existing full-refund and `charge.refunded` behaviour is untouched (`invariant-fall-cancel.spec.ts` 21 cases still green, unmodified). ## 2026-08-16 — The waiver gate was silently ending every first-time registration; the waiver is now signed in place
- **Situation:** Sam reported the fall registration as broken: "No payment after waiver completion. Then no confirmation screen after payment." Probing prod first ruled out the obvious suspects — `/fall` renders the form (`NEXT_PUBLIC_FALL_REGISTRATION_OPEN` is on), `POST /api/checkout-fall` reaches the waiver gate rather than 503ing (the Stripe price env is set), `/schedule` shows 16 live `Reserve · $20` buttons, and `/fall/success` renders. The break was in the gate's *bounce*. When `hasWaiverOnFile` is false the four paid checkout routes 409 with `code: "waiver_required"`, and all four client forms answered that with `window.location.href = data.signUrl` — a full page navigation, which discards the React state holding the entire registration. `/waiver/sign` then offered "Continue to registration →" pointing at `/fall`: the **top** of a long page, with an **empty form**. A parent who signed had to retype the group, their name, email, phone, the player's first name and birth year, the emergency contact and the allergy note, then scroll back down and start over. Almost nobody does — so a **first-time family could not reach Stripe at all**, which is every family the gate actually fires for. Not a re-prompt loop: `notion-waivers.ts` lowercases the email on both write and read, so the gate does clear once signed. The second symptom is downstream of the first — no payment, therefore no confirmation screen — but `/fall/success` did have a real latent weakness, showing the season details *only* if the live Stripe `checkout.sessions.retrieve` succeeded and otherwise falling back to a bare "You're in!" with no dates at all.
- **Decision:** Sign the waiver **without leaving the form**. On the 409 the forms now render `<InlineWaiverStep>` — the waiver copy from the existing `src/data/waiver.ts` single source of truth, plus signature + "I agree" — POST it to the unchanged `/api/waiver-sign`, and on success re-fire checkout from the payload still held in state. Chosen over the save-and-resume alternative (stash the payload, bounce, rehydrate on `?resume=1`) specifically because resume would have written the player's first name, birth year, allergies and emergency contact into browser storage — a new persistence location for minor PII — whereas signing in place persists **nothing new anywhere**; React state simply survives, which is the durable fix rather than plumbing around the symptom. Sam approved approach, scope (all four forms, not just fall) and the success-page hardening via AskUserQuestion before any code was written, per IPAV. Parent name/email/phone ride through from the registration the parent just filled in, so the step asks only for the signature; no child field is ever sent to the waiver route, keeping it parent-scoped exactly as before. The 409 discriminator moved to a new client-safe `src/lib/waiver-required.ts` (`waiver-gate.ts` now re-exports it) so the forms compare on the same string without dragging the Notion client into their browser bundles. `isWaiverRequired` deliberately **drops the old `&& data.signUrl` condition** — nothing navigates now, and requiring the link meant a missing `signUrl` dropped the parent into a raw error toast instead of the waiver.
